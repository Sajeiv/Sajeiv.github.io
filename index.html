Graph neural networks for crop genomics

−10%prediction error vs. the baseline model (MSE)

My undergraduate thesis. I compared 3–5 graph neural network architectures for predicting canola traits from genomic data, and built the data pipelines for 10,000+ genetic markers.

How it worked
Question
Does modelling relationships between plants as a graph improve genomic prediction?
Approach
Cleaned and transformed CSV data with Pandas and NumPy, then trained and tuned several GNN architectures against a baseline.
Outcome
The best model reduced MSE by about 10% over the baseline. I presented the results to faculty and wrote them up in a 25+ page thesis.
